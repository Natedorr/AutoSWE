## Must never appear

## Why this case exists

`waiting` is easy to reach from the plan phase, where asking is the expected outcome. This
case proves the same interruption works **mid-fix** — the fixer can stop, ask, and be resumed
by a plain reply, rather than guessing and committing the wrong thing.

## Backend notes

Needs mid-run tool interception, which Codex does not have. On pass B the fixer will run
straight through to `fixed` without asking. That is the documented backend limit, not a bug:
score the case **skipped** and move on.
