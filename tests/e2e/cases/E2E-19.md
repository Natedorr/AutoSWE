## Must never appear

`error`

## Why this case exists

`with <guidance>` is free text appended to the phase's prompt. It is the only way a user
steers *how* a phase works rather than *which* phase runs, and it fails quietly: the phase
still completes, the label is still right, and the instruction was simply dropped. The only
way to catch that is to read the output.

Ask for two things in step 1 on purpose — a single instruction can be met by luck.

## Soft assertions

Both steps are **soft**. Score them on substance, not on exact strings: the question is whether
the guidance is visibly reflected, not whether the model phrased it a particular way. If step 2
lands on `review_failed` or `review_blocked`, that is acceptable — the verdict is not what is
under test, the guidance plumbing is.
